# Code of conduct

KidsWatch for Plex welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels
of experience. Help others learn, ask clear questions, and discuss ideas with
care. Critique the work respectfully, accept corrections, and leave room for
people to disagree.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual comments, personal attacks, and
repeated unwanted contact are not welcome. Do not publish someone else's private
information. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests,
discussions, and other project spaces.

If you experience or witness a problem, contact a repository maintainer through
a private contact method listed on their GitHub profile. If none is available,
open a brief issue asking for a private contact method without naming anyone or
sharing incident details. Do not post private reports in public threads.

Maintainers will consider reports fairly, limit sharing of private details to
what is needed to respond, and may edit or remove content, pause a discussion,
warn someone, or restrict participation. They will choose a response based on
the behavior and its impact. These expectations also apply to maintainers.
