Limitations to know before you start

KidsWatch does a few things well. It is honest to say what it does not do.

  • It is not a device lock. {{facts.device_lock}}
  • Rules follow the child's own account. {{facts.parent_profile_note}}
  • It only covers your server. It does not filter anything played from other servers or other apps.
  • New titles take a little while. {{facts.sync_delay}}
  • Ratings come from your library. A title with a wrong rating still needs a parent to look at it.
  • {{platform}} can change. {{facts.platform_changes}}
  • One copy per database. Run a single container. It is not built to scale out.
