Start here, together

Your account for you.
Their account for them.

No child email address. No outside signup. Just a local child user on your family’s Jellyfin server.

First, the grown-ups

  1. Ask your server owner

    The person who runs Jellyfin prepares KidsWatch and sends you a private parent invitation.

  2. Sign in as yourself

    Use your existing Jellyfin identity, with Quick Connect or the available password fallback. Your server owner approves your parent access. An invitation alone is not approval.

  3. Wait for access if needed

    You may see “Waiting for owner approval.” If you do not have a Jellyfin adult account, ask the owner to create one first.

Then, your child

  1. Add a child

    Choose a nickname, content level, and limits. KidsWatch creates a local Jellyfin user and checks its restrictions.

  2. Check the status

    With trusted parents enabled, a verified child can activate automatically. Otherwise, the child stays disabled until the owner approves.

  3. Help sign in the TV

    Choose the verified child in KidsWatch. Start Quick Connect on their Jellyfin device and confirm “Sign this device in as [child].” If unavailable, use parent-assisted child login.

01 / Their library

You know your child.
You choose the titles.

Age levels and picked titles help organize the library your child browses. They are not a safety lock: a known direct video link could still fetch a blocked title in our Jellyfin 12.3 lab.

  • Little kids: G movies and preschool shows
  • Kids: G and PG movies, shows up to TV-PG
  • Teens: movies up to PG-13, shows up to TV-14
  • R, NC-17, TV-MA, unknown, and unrated are excluded from the selection rules

Choosing a series applies to the whole series, including new episodes. Episode ratings still apply; this preview does not offer episode exceptions.

02 / Their routine

Make the rule.
Leave room for life.

Daily minutes set the budget. Watch hours set when it can be used. Extra minutes do not extend bedtime; a later bedtime does not add minutes.

  • School days, weekends, and school breaks
  • One budget for each child across their devices
  • Pause for dinner or add 15 minutes for today

Try Pause and bonus time in the dashboard above ↑

Usage depends on reports from the player. If the server is offline or contact is lost, the owner needs to check it.

On their screen

Their Jellyfin app.
With a few important limits.

  • Their own local user. Sign their device in as the verified child, not as you or the server administrator.
  • A filtered library, not a safety lock. In our Jellyfin 12.3 lab, a child could still fetch video from a blocked title using a known direct video link. A title missing from the library is not securely blocked.
  • Playback can still continue or start. Turning playback off on the server did not block playback setup or direct streaming in the lab. Buffered video may also keep playing. This known server gap applies regardless of the client; it is not just an unsupported-device risk.
  • A shared TV needs care. Remove saved adult logins that a child could switch to. Previously downloaded files are not recalled by a new rule.

Companion controls, not guaranteed protection. “Watch time ended. We asked the player to stop” means a request was sent, not that viewing ended. Native controls are best effort: there is no secure title blocking or guaranteed cutoff for any client. No client is certified by this preview. Ask your owner about these known limits before family use.
