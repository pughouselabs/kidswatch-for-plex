I run a Plex server I'm a parent

KidsWatch for Plex is installed by whoever runs your family's Plex server. Parents sign in with the Plex account they already watch with. Run the server? See how to install it.

Account and playback limits

Rules follow the child's own Plex account on your server. Videos stop at the next check, normally within 20 seconds. A parent's account, other servers and other apps are outside those rules.

Read the playback limits and device results.

What it does

Set it up once, then adjust from your phone

Everything works on the Plex apps your kids already use. You make the rules in KidsWatch, and the server keeps them.

Choose exactly what they can watch

Use an allow list or block list for individual movies and shows, or switch to "Only titles I allow" for the strictest setup. Allows stop at PG-13 movies and TV-14 shows; R, NC-17, TV-MA and unrated titles stay hidden.

  • Little kids, Kids and Teens levels, based on official ratings
  • Always allow or always block a title for finer control than its age rating
  • Search by title, genre or rating
  • New titles on the server are marked New for you to review

And the little things

Made for real family life

  • Share the job

    Ask the server owner to add a co-parent or grandparent who can help manage a child.

  • Siblings in one step

    Start a new child with a brother's or sister's rules, or copy rules across any time.

  • Every screen

    Rules follow your child's Plex account on the TV, tablet, phone or browser.

  • Safe by default

    Downloads stay off, and the free online movies and Live TV from Plex are switched off on kids' accounts.

  • Who changed what

    Every change is listed with who made it and when, so co-parents stay in sync.

  • Ask the server owner

    Get a new movie or show added, ask for more access, or suggest a feature.

Getting started

Ready in three steps

  1. 1

    The server owner installs it

    It runs in Docker next to their Plex server. Your family's movies and shows stay where they are.

  2. 2

    Parents add each child

    Sign in with Plex, give each child a free Plex account, connect it with a one-time link, and pick their titles and screen time.

  3. 3

    The server owner approves

    Their titles appear in Plex, ready on any TV, tablet or phone they sign in to.
