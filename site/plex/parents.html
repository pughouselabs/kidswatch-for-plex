For parents

Your kids' own Plex, with you in charge

Watching on someone else's Plex server? Choose what your kids can watch, set screen time and bedtime, and pause it all from your phone.

Ask your server ownerTake a look around

Your controls live in your phone's web browser. There is nothing to install. Your server owner sets up KidsWatch first.

Account and playback limits

Rules follow the child's own Plex account on your server. Videos stop at the next check, normally within 20 seconds. A parent's account, other servers and other apps are outside those rules.

Their Plex, with your rules

What your kids see

  • The app they already know. The normal Plex app on their TV, tablet or phone, signed in to their own account.
  • Just their movies and shows. Only the titles you allow appear. They do not see KidsWatch itself. To them, it is just their Plex.
  • A clear stopping point. When time runs out, the video stops. If you pause watching, they see “A parent paused Plex” with the time it ends.

A quick checklist

What you need

You bring the family.
Your server owner sets it up.

  • Your own Plex account

    One that already watches from the server. You sign in to KidsWatch with it.

  • A free Plex account for each child

    Use an email address you check and keep the password to yourself. A Plex Home profile alone will not work.

  • A server owner willing to install it

    The person who runs the Plex server you watch from. They need Plex Pass and a little time to set it up.

Use your own email with a tag.

Add +<kidname>plex right before the @ in your own email address. For example, jane@gmail.com becomes jane+emmaplex@gmail.com. Mail still lands in your inbox, so you keep control of your child's account and password.

If your email provider does not support this, use any address you check.

Keep your account for you. Rules follow your child's own account. If they watch on your account, they see what you see, so put a PIN on your profile.

Give it a moment. Videos stop at the next check, usually within a minute, not the very second time runs out.
